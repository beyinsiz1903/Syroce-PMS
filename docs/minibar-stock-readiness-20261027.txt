MINIBAR / DEMO PRESENTATION READINESS — 27 OCTOBER 2026

Scope
  Central hotel warehouse integration, durable stock recovery, reviewed
  synthetic future-booking archive, and a bounded permission/configuration/
  interruption/concurrency regression matrix. Not a guarantee for every
  conceivable deployment, device, role override, or infrastructure failure.

Implemented
  1. Minibar catalog explicitly selects central inventory_items or the legacy
     inventory backend. Product identity is frozen on consumption, never
     guessed from a product name or a subsequently edited catalog.
  2. Financial consumption stores durable stock jobs in the same transaction.
     Stock quantity, one source-bound movement, sealed audit and job completion
     commit together. A stock outage never silently loses the job; retry does
     not recreate guest charges. A missing completed movement is not permission
     to deplete the same stock again.
  3. Shortage/inactive/missing/invalid stock is visible to operators. Catalog
     edits, stock opening, reconciliation and actual stock movements are audited.
     Central warehouse opening has a stable request UUID and content hash.
  4. Browser retry retains the exact consumption request across a tab reload,
     scoped to tenant and actor. Cart/room cannot be changed while the outcome
     is unknown. Invalid/unavailable request storage blocks a new HTTP write.
  5. Legacy jobless consumption is NOT assumed to have verified stock. Explicit
     reasoned reconciliation creates a NEW current-open-day stock instruction;
     original lines, guest debt, currency, VAT and closed financial day remain.
  6. Seven exact synthetic future-booking IDs may be locally archived only for
     Demo Hotel 100001, with paused outbound channels, reviewed source hash,
     reason, retained before-images and sealed logs. Any financial/service source,
     including a zero-valued source or a folio-only payment, blocks the archive.
     No provider cancellation, no-show, room deletion or accounting correction.

Verification coverage
  - Actual replica transactions: concurrent same-key requests, two rooms racing
    for the last unit, lost acknowledgements, stock/audit/financial/projection
    interruptions, immutable source identity, tenant isolation, and safe replay.
  - 13 roles x TRY/USD/EUR x full-comp/normal x open/closed folio x sufficient/
    insufficient stock: 312 combined cases, plus permission dependency checks
    for front desk, POS, SPA, reports and individual permission grants.
  - Regression suite also covers POS refund/report currency, room projections,
    source VAT, business-day precedence, SPA quotas/entitlements and audit.
  - Frontend stock/retry/read-error/access tests and the complete frontend suite.
  - Exact executed counts and remaining live acceptance are recorded in the PR.
    Old standalone HTTP check-in tests require a separately seeded local server;
    they are not claimed as executed by the replica-only regression run.
