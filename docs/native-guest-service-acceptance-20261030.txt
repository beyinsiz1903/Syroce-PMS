Source-owned fixes
Legacy room service reads persisted menu prices/currency/optional inclusive VAT.
Missing product/currency, changed quoted price, invalid quantity and missing
charge consent stop. Structured QR F&B products may be read only through the
same-tenant property's explicit booking/room link; no property is guessed.
Whole order, all line charges, balances and sealed audit are one transaction.
Stable source identity + same-intent locks prevent duplicate debt; changed
intent409. Guest ownership is checked, including the linked guest inside the
transaction; staff needs financial authority. Unknown VAT stays null, not0%.
Requested service date/time is explicit and hotel-local; actual delivered_at is
separate, stamped once through a forward-only audited transition. A charged
order cannot be hidden by cancellation before authorised charge void/refund.

Activity/function-space creation serializes capacity under resource locks.
Intervals compare instants, use half-open bounds and hotel timezone, include
overnight carry-ins and fractional hours. Ambiguous/bad source times fail closed.
Native completion requires a current persisted-tariff quote_hash, a reason,
matching currency and explicit same-tenant open folio. Activity guest must match
the target stay. Hourly/daily function tariffs are explicitly selected and UI
labels their proportional elapsed-hour/24h calculation. A tariff/calendar change
invalidates the quote. Completion, canonical charge, balances and sealed audit
are atomic; exact retry reuses the immutable snapshot, not a new tariff.
No implicit refund or old completed-source migration. Paid/completed schedules
have disabled cancellation controls; pending bookings may be cancelled normally.
Room lookup now joins the same-tenant physical room and canonical room_id,
rejects multiple active stays and never uses a conflicting legacy room number.
Read-only lookup reports multiple open windows without choosing one. Financial
callers require one unambiguous OPEN folio; no closed-folio fallback.

Mobile room service explicitly selects one of multiple active stays. Cart money
uses the product currency, never a silent TRY fallback; unknown/mixed currency,
changed price and invalid quantities are blocked. First submitted intent and
service time stay frozen through uncertain-response retries. Orders reconcile
by the persisted request_id; this is NOT an offline automatic purchase queue.
